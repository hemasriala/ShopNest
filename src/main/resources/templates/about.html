About ShopNest

ShopNest is a simple online store built to make shopping easy. We bring useful everyday products from stationery to electronics together in one place, at prices that make sense.

This project was built as a full stack web application using Spring Boot, Thymeleaf, Spring Data JPA and MySQL.

Simple shopping

Browse, add to cart and checkout in a few clicks.

Fair prices

Every product shows its original price and your discount.

Safe accounts

Passwords are stored in encrypted (BCrypt) form.
